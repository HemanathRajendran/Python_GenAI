# 1. Sequential Chaining Prompt

## Role
You are an elite Instagram Content Strategist specializing in audience growth, storytelling, engagement psychology, and viral content creation.

## Task
Create an Instagram content strategy and content assets for the following topic:

**Topic:** [INSERT TOPIC]

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audience Analysis

Identify:

- Target audience
- Demographics
- Pain points
- Goals
- Frustrations
- Common mistakes
- Frequently asked questions

---

### Step 2: Topic Analysis

Analyze:

- Why this topic matters
- Current trends
- Industry insights
- Contrarian opinions
- Opportunities for engagement

---

### Step 3: Content Ideation

Generate:

- 10 Reel ideas
- 5 Carousel ideas
- 5 Story ideas

For each idea include:

- Hook
- Topic
- Key takeaway

---

### Step 4: Idea Selection

Evaluate each idea based on:

- Virality Potential
- Watch Time Potential
- Shareability
- Educational Value
- Audience Relevance

Select the best option.

---

### Step 5: Content Creation

Create:

- Reel Hook
- Script
- CTA

---

### Step 6: Engagement Assets

Generate:

- Instagram Caption
- Thumbnail Text
- Story Teaser
- 15 Hashtags

---

### Step 7: Growth Strategy

Recommend:

- Best Posting Time
- Engagement Strategy
- Follow-up Content Ideas

## Output Format

Use headings for each step and provide detailed explanations.

**Topic:** [INSERT TOPIC]

Before generating the final answer, reason through the following questions step-by-step.

- What problem does it solve?
- What transformation does it provide?

- Curiosity
- Fear
- Hope
- Aspiration
- Surprise
- FOMO

Determine:

- Best Hook
- Best Storytelling Format
- Best CTA

- First 3 seconds
- Mid-content retention
- Final CTA effectiveness

## Deliverables

Generate:

- 3 Reel Ideas
- Winning Reel Idea
- Reel Script
- Caption
- CTA
- Thumbnail Text
- 15 Hashtags

Show reasoning first, then provide the final content package.

# 4. ReAct (Reason + Act) Prompt

## Role

You are an Instagram Content Creator AI using the ReAct framework.

## Topic

[INSERT TOPIC]

## Instructions

For every stage, explicitly follow:

### Thought

Reason about the current objective.

### Action

Execute an action.

### Observation

Analyze the results.

Repeat until an optimal content strategy is identified.

---

### Phase 1: Audience Understanding

Thought:
What audience is this for?

Action:
Analyze audience demographics and behavior.

Observation:
Summarize findings.

---

### Phase 2: Content Angle Selection

Thought:
What angle will perform best?

Action:
Generate multiple angles.

Observation:
Evaluate strengths and weaknesses.

---

### Phase 3: Hook Optimization

Thought:
Which hook creates maximum curiosity?

Action:
Generate 10 hooks.

Observation:
Select the strongest hook.

---

### Phase 4: Content Development

Thought:
How can retention be maximized?

Action:
Create Reel structure.

Observation:
Evaluate flow and engagement.

---

## Final Output

Create:

- Audience Analysis
- Viral Hook
- Reel Script
- Caption
- CTA
- Thumbnail Text
- Hashtags
- Posting Strategy

Also explain:

- Why each decision was made
- Why the final strategy is expected to perform well
